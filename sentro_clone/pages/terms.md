# Terms

Noryx is provided as a market-information interface. Data can be delayed,
incomplete, or unavailable, and it is not financial, legal, or tax advice.

You are responsible for verifying token contracts, wallet prompts, network
fees, slippage, and local legal requirements before signing any transaction.

No transaction is executed by Noryx unless a configured wallet provider
explicitly presents it to you and you approve it. You use the interface at
your own risk.
