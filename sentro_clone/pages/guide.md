# Noryx guide

Noryx is a read-only market terminal for Robinhood Chain. Use the board to
review new launches, bonding progress, and pooled markets.

## Board columns

- **New** shows recently indexed launches.
- **Almost bonded** shows curve markets approaching their threshold.
- **In pool** shows markets that have migrated to a liquidity pool.

Each row includes the token address, market stage, activity, liquidity
indicators, and available social links. Select a row to open its token view.

## Connect a wallet

Select **Sign in** to connect a browser wallet such as MetaMask, Rabby, or
another injected wallet. Noryx requests Robinhood Chain (`4663`) and stores
only the public address in the browser. Never share a seed phrase or private
key.

## Trading status

Market data is read-only until wallet signing and production transaction
providers are explicitly configured. Do not treat a quote or displayed market
row as a guarantee of execution.
