# Support

## Market data is empty

Refresh the page and check the connection status. The board depends on the
configured indexer and RPC endpoints; temporary outages can delay new rows.

## Images are missing

Token images may be hosted on IPFS or third-party CDNs. A token can remain
valid even when its image host is unavailable.

## Wallet connection problems

Confirm that your wallet is unlocked, switch to Robinhood Chain, and try
connecting again. Noryx does not have access to your wallet credentials.

## Contact

For deployment or integration support, provide the browser console error and
the affected public contract address. Never send secrets.
